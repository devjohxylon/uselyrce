Get started Pricing

Features

Admin panel

See who is online, kick or ban players, send broadcasts, check Discord-linked accounts, and run RCON commands from your browser.

Kit builder

Select items from the list to build a kit, then save it. Players claim kits in-game through quick chats configured in the panel.

Discord bot

Included with every plan. Invite it to your Discord for population channels, kill feeds, and related RCE bot tools. Staff can also sign into the panel with Discord.

Your URL

Each workspace gets a dedicated panel address, such as astral.usely.dev. Owners sign in with email; staff sign in with Discord.

Multi-server

Pro and Network support more than one WebRCON connection. Switch the active server from the panel header.
