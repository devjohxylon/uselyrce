How communities get set up

Three steps from checkout to live panel.

1 · Subscribe

Pick a plan and pay. We email you a setup link to choose your panel address — like astral.usely.dev — and your password.

2 · Servers

Paste Nitrado WebRCON host, port, and password. Switch servers from the panel header.

3 · Staff roles

Invite the bot, then map Discord roles to permissions. Staff sign in to your panel with Discord.
