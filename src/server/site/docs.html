Setup, servers, kits, Discord, and billing.

How it works

Usely is hosted. You add WebRCON once; we keep the connection for the panel and Discord bot.

  • Panel — players, kits, bans, RCON.
  • Discord bot — feeds, pop channels, staff tools.
  • Workspace — your servers and billing at *.usely.dev.
Create your workspace
  1. Pick a plan on pricing and pay.
  2. Open the setup link from email (7 days).
  3. Choose a panel address, owner password, Discord, and WebRCON.
  4. Create staff keys under Workspace → Staff keys.

Pick an address you’ll keep — staff bookmarks depend on it.

Connect a server
Host
WebRCON IP or hostname.
Port
WebRCON port (not the game port).
Password
RCON password — stored encrypted.

Values come from your host’s control panel. Offline servers retry automatically.

Players and moderation
  • Kick / ban with a reason
  • Ban list + unban (including prior server bans)
  • Broadcast
  • Linked Discord accounts
  • Raw RCON console (separate permission)
Kits

Build kits from the Console item catalog. Players claim via quick-chat phrases you set. Kits are workspace-scoped.

Discord bot

Invite from Workspace → Setup (Manage Server required), then link the guild.

  • Pop channel rename
  • Killfeed and other feeds
  • Slash commands

Panel stays up if the bot is offline.

Staff permissions

Staff use access keys from Workspace → Staff keys. Permissions are checked per action. Billing and credentials stay on the owner login.

Multiple servers

Basic = 1, Pro = 2, Network = more. Switch servers from the panel header. Kits and staff keys are shared. Downgrade only after disconnecting extras.

Billing

Monthly via Stripe. Change plan, payment method, invoices, and cancel from the panel. Upgrades apply immediately; cancel keeps access until period end.

Troubleshooting

Server offline

Check WebRCON host, port, and password.

Online but empty player list

Usually nobody is on. If they are, confirm full RCON access on the host.

Bot silent

Confirm it’s in the server and can post / rename the assigned channels.

Staff can’t sign in

They must use your panel URL and a full enabled ask_… key.

Still stuck? FAQ or contact with your panel address.
